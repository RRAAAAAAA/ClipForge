ClipForge

Paste a YouTube link or upload your own footage. Get vertical clips with beat-synced B-roll, music, titles and scores you can edit.

Your video gets beat-synced B-roll, upbeat music and no dead air. Someone must be talking: the clips are picked from what is said.

B-roll footage (optional) Upload one or more videos of your own (cars, wins, money, teaching, ...). ClipForge finds the scenes in them and uses them as B-roll where they fit what is said. Anything that does not fit comes from Pixabay stock.
